About

Analyst by training, builder by habit.

I hold a Level 7 qualified MSc (equivalent to a Master's degree) in Big Data Analytics, awarded with Distinction, and I got there the same way I approach everything — by actually building the thing, not just reading about it. My dissertation merged three real‑world data sources into one 50,000‑record dataset, engineered over 100 features, and used SHAP to make an XGBoost model's reasoning legible to people who don't care about hyperparameters.

Before that, I built and shipped live web features as a Junior Web Developer — full MERN stack, RESTful APIs, and a Salesforce CRM keeping client data honest. I'm a genuine, everyday user of Claude Code and other AI tools, not because it's fashionable, but because it's how I actually get things done faster without cutting corners.

Outside of formal projects, I keep building: a personal Power BI project analysing UK housing prices, and time spent at a Google-hosted networking event and a Cognizant event in London, staying close to where the industry is heading.
